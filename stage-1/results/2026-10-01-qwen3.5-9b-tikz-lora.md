the 2x8 shape while preserving the same effective batch size.

## Publication and interpretation

The public Hugging Face repository was verified as public with all expected
base weights, tokenizer files, unmerged LoRA files, checksums, metrics, job
history, resolved configuration, and provenance. Its portable adapter config
points to the pinned upstream base revision and records rank/alpha 64/64.

Validation loss is token-level cross-entropy against one reference TikZ
implementation. It does not directly measure compilation or rendered-image
similarity; those require separate compile/render and qualitative evaluation.
